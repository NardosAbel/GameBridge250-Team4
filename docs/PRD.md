# Product Requirements Document

## Product vision

Build Ethiopia’s integrated gaming and esports platform: a trusted ecosystem where students, gamers, teams, schools, universities, gaming zones, organizers, sponsors, and technology learners can connect, compete, learn, and grow.

## Target users

- University and school students
- Casual and competitive gamers
- Team captains and managers
- Tournament organizers
- Gaming zones and venues
- Sponsors and brands
- Content creators
- Moderators and platform administrators

## Value proposition

- A verified gaming identity for every player.
- A reliable tournament system with transparent results and rankings.
- A community layer for teams, schools, universities, and local gaming zones.
- Secure event ticketing and QR-code entry.
- Digital-awareness and technology-learning opportunities.
- A sponsorship marketplace with measurable audience value.

## MVP scope

1. Student registration and verification
2. University and team profiles
3. Tournament creation and registration
4. Brackets, schedules, results, and disputes
5. Rankings and leaderboards
6. Notifications
7. Basic moderation
8. Paid registration or ticketing through Telebirr/Chapa
9. Basic digital-awareness content

## Success metrics

- Verified student registrations
- Active weekly users
- Teams created
- Tournaments created and completed
- Match completion rate
- Dispute rate and resolution time
- Ticket sales and check-in rate
- University participation
- Sponsor leads and sponsored events
- Learning completion rate
